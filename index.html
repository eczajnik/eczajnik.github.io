Towards the stars

I'm optimistic about what comes next.

I support accelerationism and e/acc, especially eu/acc. I believe that the faster we develop AI, the sooner we'll be able to tackle climate change, cure diseases, overcome death, and reach the stars.

A big part of that outlook comes from Iain M. Banks's Culture series: stories that helped me imagine a future of abundance, curiosity, and possibility. If that sounds like your kind of future, I highly recommend his books.
